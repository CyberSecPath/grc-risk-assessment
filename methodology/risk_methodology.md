# Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Scope

This simulated assessment considers the business processes and technology of Northstar Office Supply, a fictional 25-person online office-supply retailer. It focuses on employee identities, company laptops, email, customer/order information, backups, and third-party services.

## 2. Assessment steps

1. Identify business-critical assets and information.
2. Identify plausible threats and weaknesses.
3. Estimate inherent likelihood and impact.
4. Document relevant existing controls.
5. Estimate residual risk after considering those controls.
6. Recommend a risk treatment action, owner role, and target date.
7. Summarize the most important issues for management.

## 3. Likelihood scale

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Unusual; would require exceptional circumstances |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could occur, but not expected in normal conditions |
| 3 | Possible | A realistic scenario that may occur |
| 4 | Likely | Expected to occur in many circumstances |
| 5 | Almost certain | Expected to occur frequently or imminently |

## 4. Impact scale

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Insignificant | Negligible disruption or loss |
| 2 | Minor | Limited disruption, quickly recoverable |
| 3 | Moderate | Noticeable business disruption or manageable data exposure |
| 4 | Major | Significant operational, financial, or confidentiality impact |
| 5 | Severe | Extended outage, major financial loss, or serious sensitive-data exposure |

## 5. Scoring and priority

**Risk score = Likelihood × Impact**

| Score | Priority |
|---:|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Moderate |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

Inherent risk is assessed before considering controls. Residual risk is an estimate after considering the controls listed in the register. A residual score is not proof that a control is effective; it is a reasoned estimate for this fictional exercise.

## 6. Treatment options

- **Mitigate:** Add or improve controls to reduce likelihood or impact.
- **Accept:** Management knowingly retains the risk within its tolerance.
- **Transfer:** Shift part of the financial impact through insurance or contractual arrangements.
- **Avoid:** Stop the activity that creates the risk.

## 7. Assumptions and limitations

All company details, control descriptions, owners, dates, and scores are fictional. No interviews, evidence collection, technical testing, or independent validation occurred. A real assessment would require stakeholder interviews, evidence review, system configuration checks, and management approval.
